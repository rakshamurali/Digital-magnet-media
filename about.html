ORIGIN SCRIPT

Forged in Bengaluru. Proven across the globe.

"We believe the best marketing campaigns should look and feel as memorable as your favorite film."

Founded 2016 • Indiranagar, Bengaluru

Digital Magnet Media was founded in 2016 on a singular conviction: modern digital marketing had become uninspired, automated, and numbingly generic. Brands were drowning in cold metrics without narrative substance, while traditional agencies produced pretty collateral that failed to move the revenue needle.

We merged the cinematic storytelling tradition of Indian creative culture with the analytical rigor of performance engineering. By treating every campaign as a theatrical release—with high-stakes narrative arcs, thumb-stopping hooks, and conversion architectures—we turned passive scrollers into passionate brand advocates.

Today, operating across our creative soundstage in Bengaluru and international client desk in New York, we steward high-velocity growth for challenger tech scale-ups, D2C disrupters, and global enterprises alike.

"In marketing, as in cinema, greatness happens when fearless strategy meets unforgettable craft."

— Digital Magnet Media Credo
